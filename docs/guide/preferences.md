# Preferences

Open Actions and choose the **Settings** tab. Every change takes effect at once and is saved with your other settings.

## Interface

- **Light interface** switches the interface to the light theme. The gallery is always dark.
- **Right-hand interface** moves the sidebar to the right edge.
- **Brush cursor** draws the outline of the brush under the pen.

## Gestures

- **Eyedropper on hold** turns the hold-to-pick-a-colour gesture on or off.
- **Hold to Shape** turns the snapping of strokes to shapes on or off.
- **Undo and redo gestures** turns the two- and three-finger taps on or off.
- **Repeat undo on hold** keeps undoing while two fingers are held.
- **Two-finger pan, zoom, rotate** turns two-finger navigation on or off.
- **Gesture controls** opens the full sheet. There you can turn each touch gesture on or off, including:
  - **Disable touch painting** for pen-only work, where fingers never paint but still navigate and undo;
  - the **Eyedropper delay** (0.1 to 1.5 seconds) and the **Hold to Shape delay** (0.2 to 1.5 seconds);
  - the three-finger scrub that clears a layer, the three-finger swipe that opens the copy menu, and the four-finger tap that hides the interface.

## Pressure curve

The pressure curve shapes how touch force and pen pressure become the brush's pressure. Drag its two middle points to bend the curve. Points stay in order, so more pressure never gives less effect. **Reset** puts the curve back to a straight line.

## Timelapse

Timelapse recording is on for new documents by default. See Animation and timelapse.
